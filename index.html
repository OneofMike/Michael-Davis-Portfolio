I tend to enter unfamiliar problems by looking for structure first. I want to understand the environment, identify what actually matters, and see how the pieces connect before I build anything.

The projects here come from different industries, but the process behind them stays consistent: research, organize, test, simplify, and turn what I learn into something usable.

This portfolio is less about one specific subject and more about showing how I think when I’m given something new to figure out.
